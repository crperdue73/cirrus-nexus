WHAT I INSTALLED (backend/main.py, backup main.py.bak-2026-09-27-ghostreap)
  - `_ghost_reap_loop()`: every NEXUS_GHOST_REAP_SECONDS (default 120) it lists
    live containers, drops any registered session with zero containers matching
    its id, and best-effort releases that session's ttyd ports.
  - started from a new @app.on_event("startup") hook.
  - DISABLE ANY TIME: NEXUS_GHOST_REAP=0 . Tune: NEXUS_GHOST_REAP_SECONDS.
  - All failures swallowed + logged; the reaper can never take the app down.
  - Uses the existing docker-ps-by-name check; NO new grading/routing semantics.
