--- THE BUG ---
Naive SRL IRB config puts access ports in mac-vrf-1 and irb0.0 in ip-vrf-1
ONLY. The L2 bridge domain and the L3 gateway end up in DIFFERENT network
instances. Result: PC-to-PC works (same L2), but the switch's own IP
10.0.1.254 never answers ARP -> 100% loss (observed).

--- THE FIX ---
1. irb0.0 must be a member of BOTH mac-vrf-1 (L2) AND ip-vrf-1 (L3).
2. On a fresh switch, access subinterfaces default to 'type routed';
   they must be explicitly 'type bridged' to join a mac-vrf.
Config shipped in lab-definitions/srl-demo/assets/sw1.cfg
