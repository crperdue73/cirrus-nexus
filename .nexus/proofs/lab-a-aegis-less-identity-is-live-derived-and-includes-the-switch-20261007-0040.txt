THE REMAINING GAP (a design decision, flagged for Dad, NOT auto-fixed): unlike the FRR
substrate, which is pinned (assets/frr-image.pin) and asserted at install, the SR Linux
image is PULLED and NOT pinned. So while a silent `docker pull` of a new :latest produces a
different-but-HONEST digest, nothing flags it as "this is not the image the lab was authored
against" — there is no expected value to compare to. Closing that would mean pinning the
SR Linux image digest and asserting on start; that changes product behaviour and is Dad's call.
