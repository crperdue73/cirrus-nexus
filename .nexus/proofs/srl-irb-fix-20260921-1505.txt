--- BEFORE (broken): pc-a -> switch IRB 10.0.1.254 ---
3 packets transmitted, 0 received, 100% packet loss   <-- IRB dead

--- ROOT CAUSE (network-instance split-brain) ---
Bridged access ports ethernet-1/1.0, ethernet-1/2.0 were bound to
network-instance mac-vrf-1 (L2 only). The IRB irb0.0 was bound ONLY to
ip-vrf-1 (L3). The L2 bridge domain and the L3 gateway were in DIFFERENT
network instances, so the switch had no L3 presence on 10.0.1.0/24.
PC-to-PC L2 worked; the switch's own IP never answered ARP.

--- FIX ---
Give irb0.0 DUAL membership: keep it in ip-vrf-1 (L3) AND add it to
mac-vrf-1 (L2). The IRB then bridges into the access VLAN and routes in
the ip-vrf. Commands committed to sw1:
  set / network-instance mac-vrf-1 interface irb0.0
